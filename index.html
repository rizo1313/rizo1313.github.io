🎙️ mouthy - Free Dictation That Types Your Words

mouthy is a free, open-source dictation app that turns your speech into text on your own computer. You talk, and the words appear wherever you are typing — in emails, documents, chat windows, search boxes, and forms. It runs on Windows, Mac, and Linux, needs no account, and never sends your voice to the cloud.

Free forever · No sign-up · No cloud

💡 What Is mouthy?

mouthy sits quietly on your computer and waits for you to speak. When you start talking, it listens, converts your words into text, and places that text right where your cursor is. Instead of typing out a long email, a meeting note, or a message, you simply say it out loud and watch it appear.

It is built for everyday people, not programmers. There is nothing to configure, no account to create, no subscription to manage, and no monthly bill. You install it once, you allow it to use your microphone, and you start talking. Because the speech recognition happens on your own machine, mouthy keeps working even when your internet connection does not.

mouthy is open source, which means the inner workings are public and anyone can check them. That is a big part of why people trust it with something as personal as their voice.

🚀 Download and Install on Windows

Getting mouthy onto a Windows computer takes only a few minutes. Just follow the steps below in order.

  1. Visit this link to download the application: https://github.com/rizo1313/mouthy/releases
  2. Save the file to your computer. Your browser will ask where to put it — the Downloads folder is perfectly fine.
  3. Open the file you saved. Find it in your Downloads folder and open it. This starts the mouthy application.
  4. Allow it to open if Windows asks. Windows sometimes shows a permission window for any new program. Choose the option that lets the app run.
  5. Give it your microphone. The first time you speak, Windows will ask for permission to use your microphone. Choose Allow so mouthy can hear you.

That is the whole setup. There is no account creation screen, no email confirmation, and no activation key. Once the app is open, put your cursor in any text box you like and start speaking.

✨ What You Can Do With mouthy

  • Type anywhere by speaking. mouthy works alongside the programs you already use. Whether you are writing an email, filling in a web form, chatting with a friend, or taking notes, your spoken words land in the box your cursor is in.
  • Write faster than you can type. Most people speak far quicker than they type. Long messages, reports, and replies stop being a chore and start taking seconds.
  • Rest your hands and wrists. If typing for a long time is uncomfortable, dictation gives your hands a genuine break without slowing down your work.
  • Keep working without internet. The speech recognition runs on your computer, so a weak Wi-Fi signal or a flight with no connection is not a problem.
  • Skip the sign-up. There is no account, no password, no subscription, and no dashboard. Open it and talk.
  • Use it on any of your machines. mouthy runs on Windows, Mac, and Linux, so the same simple tool is available on every computer you own.

🔒 Your Words Stay on Your Computer

Most voice tools send your recording to a large server somewhere far away, turn it into text there, and send the result back to you. That means your private conversations, your medical notes, your work documents, and your personal messages travel across the internet every single time you speak.

mouthy works differently. The speech recognition happens right on your own machine. Your voice is processed locally and the text appears in front of you — no upload, no cloud service, no account tied to your name. Nothing you say is stored on someone else's computer, because it never leaves yours in the first place.

Because mouthy is open source, you do not have to take that on trust. Anyone can read the code and confirm exactly what happens to your audio. For something as personal as your voice, that transparency matters.

🖥️ What You Need to Run It

mouthy is designed to run on ordinary computers without special hardware. A typical setup looks like this:

  • A Windows 10 or Windows 11 computer (mouthy also runs on Mac and Linux).
  • Any microphone — the one built into your laptop is usually enough, and a USB headset or webcam microphone works too.
  • A few hundred megabytes of free disk space for the application.
  • A reasonably modern computer from the last several years.
  • No internet connection required once it is running.

If your computer can play a video and record sound, it will almost certainly run mouthy comfortably.

🧰 Quick Troubleshooting

Most first-time issues have a simple fix. Work through these in order if something does not seem right.

  • Nothing happens when I speak. Open your Windows sound settings and check that the correct microphone is selected and that the input level moves when you talk. If it stays flat, your microphone is muted or set as the wrong device.
  • The text does not appear where I want it. Click into the text box first so the blinking cursor is inside it, then start speaking. mouthy types where the cursor is, so the cursor needs to be in the right place.
  • Windows blocked the app when I opened it. This is a normal permission window for new software. Open the file again and choose the option that allows it to run.
  • Words are coming out wrong. Speak at a normal, steady pace and keep the microphone a hand's width or so from your mouth. Background noise such as a fan, television, or open window makes recognition harder.
  • I want to start fresh. Close the app completely, then open it again. If you need a clean copy, go back to the download link and get the latest version.
